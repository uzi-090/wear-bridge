## Requested call behavior update

The user requested brief phone-handled acknowledgements, without a timer, for cellular, WhatsApp, FaceTime and other calls. The implementation follows ANCS call categories and the action labels supplied by iOS rather than an app-name allowlist.

- An active call without a matching watch Answer shows **“Answered on iPhone”** briefly. It has no duration, caller details or End Call control, and does not produce a later duration summary.
- A ringing notification removed without a watch action shows **“Call handled on iPhone”** briefly. It deliberately does not claim that the phone rejected the call: rejection, missed calls and caller hang-up are indistinguishable in the removal event.
- A matching watch Answer retains the connected-call screen and its timer. Origin is also recorded for notification buttons and generic detail actions. Failed/unavailable Answer cannot retain a watch timer. Different providers/callers cannot inherit an earlier Answer.
- Active calls are removed from the generic notification inbox. Only watch-answered calls receive an ongoing call notification. Phone-answered calls therefore cannot leave an alternate End Call action in that surface.
- Same-UID ringing→active/missed category changes now clear obsolete call state. A stale ringing call cleared during disconnect does not get a misleading phone-handled acknowledgement.

**API limit:** an exact **“Rejected using phone”** outcome is **NOT POSSIBLE WITH CURRENT PUBLIC API** from ANCS removal alone. Active category 12 is the existing experimentally observed iOS extension, not a documented guarantee across providers or iOS versions. If a provider supplies no active event, Bridge can only show the generic acknowledgement. ANCS does not supply a stable cross-notification call identity; matching provider/caller reduces incorrect origin assignment but does not establish complete concurrent-call support. [Apple ANCS specification](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/CoreBluetooth/Reference/AppleNotificationCenterServiceSpecification/Specification/Specification.html).

## Priority findings and recommended updates

P1 means fix before public release. P2 means the next reliability/UX increment. “Source review” means the failure path exists in code but was not induced against personal device data.

### F1 — P1: automatic watch reboot recovery crashes

**Real-device failure.** At 21:44:23 the boot receiver threw `ForegroundServiceStartNotAllowedException`; Bridge had no running process afterward. Opening Bridge manually restored it. The relevant calls are `BridgeService.kt:93` and `BootReceiver.onReceive` at line 102. [Crash evidence](qa-2026-10-06/watch-boot-crash.log).

Update service startup to handle ineligible starts without crashing, retain a recoverable state, and provide an actionable “Open Bridge on your watch” path. Establish the eligible background/companion-device startup route on supported firmware. Catching the exception alone does not prove automatic reconnect works. Android documents exemptions and restrictions; do not infer that `connectedDevice` is categorically banned from boot because this firmware rejected this particular start. [Android background FGS rules](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/fgs/restrictions-bg-start).

Acceptance: reboot the installed optimized build repeatedly with phone locked/unlocked and verify process, advertising, READY and secure settings recovery without manually opening Bridge.

### F2 — P1: incomplete contact transactions can replace a valid book

**Reproduced against production `ContactsChannel` with synthetic records and authenticated-frame boundary stubs.** Valid BEGIN/UPSERT/END and interrupted-disconnect retention pass. BEGIN expecting one record followed by END without UPSERT commits an empty book. A malformed UPSERT is caught, but staging survives and END still commits it. BEGIN's count is logged but not validated. `ContactsChannel.kt:52–71`.

Add a transaction ID, operation counts and a final digest; abort staging on decoding, framing or authentication failure; reject incomplete END; acknowledge only after successful persistence. Cover deletes as well as upserts and keep the previous committed generation on failure. The harness exercises channel logic, not real authentication or filesystem behavior. [Four probes](qa-2026-10-06/contact-audit.xml).

Acceptance: missing, duplicated, malformed, interleaved, disconnected and corrupted transactions never replace the previous book or publish a successful generation.

### F3 — P1: disabling contacts offline is not a durable removal request

**Source review.** `ContactSync.turnOff` sends CLEAR once and resets local state (`ContactSync.swift:140`). `WatchLink.send` silently returns without a secure session; later `watchReported` returns immediately when disabled. Consequently turning sync off while disconnected can leave contacts on the watch after reconnect, despite the UI promising their removal. A running sync also lacks a retained cancellation task and enabled/generation checks after `await refreshAvailable` and batch yields (`208–245`), allowing writes after a clear.

Persist a pending-clear operation, cancel/invalidate active sync work, serialize CLEAR after cancellation, and retry until the watch acknowledges an empty generation. Show “Removal pending — reconnect your watch” until confirmed. Do not discard the retry intent when the toggle is off.

Acceptance: turn sync off during disconnect, contact fetch, every batch position and reconnect; the book becomes empty and stays empty, while UI accurately reports pending/confirmed removal.

### F4 — P1: a late GATT callback can complete the wrong operation

**Reproduced.** After request 1 times out, its late READ callback completes request 2 of the same kind as success with request 1's value. `GattOpQueue.complete` matches only `Kind` (`GattOpQueue.kt:41`). [Probe result](qa-2026-10-06/audit-regressions.xml).

Correlate callbacks with the characteristic/descriptor and GATT session epoch. Close/rebuild the session on timeout because a late callback for the same attribute remains ambiguous. Reject callbacks from obsolete GATT objects and fail pending work on teardown.

Acceptance: timeout+late callback, reconnect+old callback and same-attribute retry cannot report another request as successful.

### F5 — P1: fresh lint fails for both watch build variants

`BridgeTileService.kt:216` uses experimental `TEXT_OVERFLOW_MARQUEE` without the required ProtoLayout opt-in. Mark the deliberate use locally or use a stable overflow mode. Then resolve material lint warnings without globally suppressing the check. Stale earlier lint artifacts reported no errors; this report uses freshly generated reports.

Acceptance: `:app:lintDebug :app:lintRelease` both pass in CI on a clean checkout.

### F6 — P2: media tile becomes inconsistent after phone player closes

**User-reproduced hardware failure.** The full media screen was correct, while the tile kept stale song/playback state. Logs show player change at 22:01:34, supported commands 9→2, PAUSED, then `TOGGLE_PLAY_PAUSE NOT_SUPPORTED` at 22:01:43.

Source review provides concrete issues to fix: tile previous/play/next controls are always clickable (`BridgeTileService.kt:224–244`); the tile sends TOGGLE unconditionally; `TileRefresher` ignores supported-command changes and relies on a debounced renderer refresh. Both tile and full screen should use one media presentation model. The full screen also enables play/pause when PLAY or PAUSE exists but always sends TOGGLE (`MediaScreen.kt:194,233`); select the supported command for the current state. `ConnectionManager` logs command results without publishing user feedback. These are confirmed code issues; renderer throttling, click timing or cached layout could also contribute to the observed stale tile, so the precise refresh root cause is not established.

Include player identity, supported controls, playback state and metadata validity in the tile refresh key. Disable unsupported actions; show “Open media on iPhone” or “No active player”; update on tile entry; expose pending/failed command feedback. Measure request, render and AMS update timestamps before adding background polling. [Android tile interaction API](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/tiles/interactions), [Tile update requests](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/wear/tiles/TileUpdateRequester).

Acceptance: play→pause→app force-close→different player→reopen, with tile and full screen matching after each transition; rapid taps, screen-off and unsupported-command changes do not leave misleading controls.

### F7 — P2: “Last synced” can mean sent rather than committed

**Source review.** iPhone contacts digests, generation and `lastSynced` are updated as soon as frames are enqueued (`ContactSync.swift:247–256`). There is no explicit committed result. The 30-second awaiting-generation deadline is only evaluated when another status arrives; it is not an active timeout job, so subsequent edits can remain blocked after a lost acknowledgement.

Separate desired, pending and acknowledged generations. Update last-confirmed timestamp on matching commit ACK and schedule a real retry timeout. Show sending/confirmed/waiting/retry states. Also distinguish the Home screen's last status receipt from last notification or contact sync.

### F8 — P2: contact permission/read and storage failure handling

**Source review.** Contacts authorization is not refreshed on every Settings return. `try? enumerateContacts` treats a read failure as an empty result (`ContactSync.swift:281`), which can cause a destructive empty diff. On watch, `renameTo`'s success result is ignored and generation is stored separately (`ContactsStore.kt:53–56,71–90`). Failed replacement or an interrupted file/preferences sequence can publish a generation inconsistent with durable records.

Refresh authorization on scene activation, distinguish a successful empty book from an error, and preserve confirmed state on fetch failure. Persist encrypted contacts and generation together using a checked atomic write. Return a failure ACK when storage fails. Do not acknowledge success merely because in-memory contacts changed.

### F9 — P2: settings changes do not immediately reconcile existing notifications

**Source review.** Rules are consulted when ANCS events arrive; the engine does not observe rule changes to rebuild existing repository entries and posted notifications. Enabling Hide content or turning an app off can leave already-visible content unchanged until another event/replay. `NotificationEngine.kt:84–137`.

Apply changed policies immediately to the inbox, history and posted mirrors, with no repeat haptic. Masked apps should immediately lose retained history. Reconcile privacy rules before acknowledging the change to the companion.

### F10 — P2: history expiration is lazy; deletion survives only the process

**Reproduced.** After advancing the test clock beyond 24 hours without a mutation, `get` still returns expired history. Expiration currently happens on upsert/remove, not reads/idle UI (`NotificationRepository.kt:109,172`). Expire when reading/entering the UI and schedule the nearest retained item's deadline while needed.

A separate probe shows watch-only deleted UIDs are forgotten after process recreation. This matches the documented process-only design; it is a UX persistence gap, not a newly violated contract. If deletion should remain stable over restarts, retain bounded UID tombstones per phone identity with TTL and no notification text. Otherwise explain the restart behavior clearly.

### F11 — P2: reconnect cleanup assumes replay completes within five seconds

**Source plus timing evidence.** Stale entries are purged five seconds after subscription (`NotificationEngine.onSubscribed`). This device replay took over 20 seconds while fetching many existing notifications. Items may temporarily become “cleared” or disappear before their valid replay arrives.

Use ANCS backlog completion/quiescence to determine reconciliation, with a bounded fallback and fresh-call/message priority. Retain the silent replay and notification quota cap. Test replay of 50+ entries with a fresh call arriving mid-backlog.

### F12 — P2: transport failure reporting and teardown need hardening

**Source review.** iPhone framed writes are all enqueued with response, but no completion queue or propagated error exists (`WatchLink.swift:257–264,650`). Size is queried for without-response while writes use with-response. Watch notification queue has no callback deadline, ignores callback status, drops the whole pending queue when one enqueue fails, and is not explicitly reset in `close` (`BridgePeripheral.kt:81–112,156,244`).

Use bounded write queues with completion/error propagation, the matching write-type size, session cancellation and transaction-aware retry. Add a timeout/status policy to the server queue and reset it during teardown. Apple documents with-response completion through `didWriteValueFor`; use that result to drive progress and failure handling. [CoreBluetooth writes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/corebluetooth/cbperipheral/writevalue(_:for:type:)), [Completion callback](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/corebluetooth/cbperipheraldelegate/1518823-peripheral).

## UI/UX recommendations

1. **Make connection readiness specific.** Distinguish Bluetooth connected, notifications ready, media ready and companion secure. Show stale battery/status values with an “Updated … ago” timestamp. After boot failure, say “Open Bridge on your watch” before suggesting re-pairing.
2. **Fix the media tile first.** It is the quick interaction surface users rely on. Its song, icon, play state and available buttons must match the full screen; unavailable controls need visible explanations, and failed taps need brief feedback.
3. **Use confirmed contact states.** “2 selected” and “2 confirmed on watch” describe different facts. Show pending additions/removals and progress based on acknowledged batches. Preserve search/favorites when permission or connection changes.
4. **Correct contradictory capability copy.** Calls currently says “Calling from the watch: Not available,” although contacts implements a call handoff requiring iPhone confirmation. Say “Request a call — confirm on iPhone.” Label generic capabilities “Supported” instead of “Works” when currently disconnected.
5. **Make privacy wording consistent.** Onboarding says “Nothing is sent to the internet,” while Settings accurately discloses App Store icon lookup by app ID. Reuse that disclosure; keep the existing user-selected always-on official-icon behavior. Notification/contact content remains local.
6. **Disable unavailable onboarding actions.** In the simulator's Bluetooth-unavailable state, Find Watch remains enabled and silently does nothing. Disable it with an actionable reason, and provide a consistent retry/Settings path for denied/off states. [Observed screen](qa-2026-10-06/onboarding-bluetooth-unavailable.png).
7. **Update Connection Guide claims.** “Reconnects … even after a restart” conflicts with this boot failure. “Only … in the background” and “silent vibrates instead” omit foreground Find iPhone playback behavior. Keep foreground/background/force-quit limits in one shared explanation.
8. **Clarify notification actions.** Keep watch-only Delete and iPhone Clear visibly distinct. Explain that enabling history keeps cleared items for up to 24 hours and process restart clears in-memory history. Explain Standard/Subtle before the picker; Samsung chooses actual popup presentation.
9. **Improve app rule discovery.** Add search and useful grouping to the iPhone notification app list, with a visible default-hidden marker and pending/applying state. The current unsearchable list becomes hard to manage after many apps notify.
10. **Finish the launch identity.** The physical iPhone Spotlight result shows a blank Bridge icon; project configuration leaves AppIcon empty. Add the approved icon asset in required sizes and consistent app naming/version display.
